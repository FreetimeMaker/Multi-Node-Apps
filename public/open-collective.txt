1. PREAMBLE AND SCOPE

These Terms of Fiscal Hosting ("Terms") govern the relationship between [Fiscal Host Name] (the "Fiscal Host") and projects, initiatives, or informal groups without their own legal personality (each a "Hosted Project" or "Project") that apply for financial and administrative hosting by the Fiscal Host.

The Fiscal Host makes its legal entity, banking infrastructure, and administrative capacity available so that a Project can receive donations, sponsorships, or other funds and incur expenses without needing to establish its own legal entity (association, nonprofit corporation, foundation, etc.).

----------------------------------------------------

2. DEFINITIONS

- Fiscal Host: The legal entity that assumes administrative and financial responsibility for the Project.
- Hosted Project: The initiative operating under the Fiscal Host's legal umbrella without its own legal personality.
- Project Lead(s): The person(s) designated by the Project to act as the point of contact with the Fiscal Host.
- Custodial Account: The account maintained by the Fiscal Host through which the Project's funds flow.

----------------------------------------------------

3. APPLICATION PROCESS

3.1 An application for fiscal hosting must be submitted in writing (via form or email) and must include at minimum:
   - The Project's name and purpose
   - Names and contact details of the Project Lead(s)
   - Planned activities and estimated annual financial volume
   - Details of existing revenue sources (donations, sponsorships, grants)

3.2 The Fiscal Host will review the application in particular for:
   - Compatibility with the Fiscal Host's own charitable/non-profit purpose
   - Legal and tax permissibility of the planned activities
   - Reputational and compliance risks

3.3 The Fiscal Host reserves the right to decline applications without stating a reason. There is no entitlement to acceptance.

3.4 Upon acceptance of the application, a Fiscal Hosting Agreement is formed between the Fiscal Host and the Project, based on these Terms.

----------------------------------------------------

4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE FISCAL HOST

The Fiscal Host agrees to:
   - Manage incoming donations and funds of the Project separately (e.g., via sub-account or cost center)
   - Process the Project's expense requests promptly, within the limits of available funds
   - Issue donation receipts where legally required and applicable
   - Provide the Project with regular (at least quarterly) account statements or financial reports
   - Enter into contracts on behalf of the Project only after consultation with the Project Lead(s)

----------------------------------------------------

5. OBLIGATIONS OF THE HOSTED PROJECT

The Project and its Project Lead(s) agree to:
   - Submit expense requests complete with supporting documentation
   - Use funds exclusively for the purpose stated in the application and approved by the Fiscal Host
   - Notify the Fiscal Host promptly of material changes to Project activities, Project Leads, or legal status
   - Not enter into contracts, obligations, or commitments on behalf of the Fiscal Host without prior written consent
   - Cooperate with audits or evidentiary requirements of the Fiscal Host (e.g., toward tax authorities) as needed
